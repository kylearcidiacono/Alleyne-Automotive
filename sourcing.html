How sourcing works

  1. 1

    Your brief

    Make, model, specification, mileage range, budget and when you need the car.

  2. 2

    The search

    We look for cars that match and tell you what we find, including anything that does not fit your brief.

  3. 3

    Your decision

    You decide whether to view or go ahead. Nothing is agreed until you confirm in writing.

What to tell us

  • The make and model you want, or two or three you would consider
  • Specification and options that matter to you
  • Your budget and the mileage you are happy with
  • Colour preferences, and anything you want to avoid
  • When you need the car
  • Whether you have a car to part exchange

Who you are buying from

From our own stock

Where a car is ours, we sell it to you as the seller and you buy from Alleyne Automotive Ltd.

Sourced on your behalf

We look for a car to match your brief. We confirm who is selling the car and the agreed terms in writing before you commit.
