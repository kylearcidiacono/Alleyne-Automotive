How sourcing works

  1. 1

    Your brief and initial fee

    Tell us the car, specification and budget. We agree the initial fee and sourcing terms with you before the search begins.

  2. 2

    We find and check

    We search for a suitable vehicle and view and check it on your behalf. The initial fee covers the search, including our time and travel.

  3. 3

    View and decide

    When we find a suitable car, we arrange for you to view it. If you are happy to proceed, the final sourcing fee is payable on the agreed terms. We handle the purchase arrangements and handover with the seller.

Clear sourcing fees

There is an initial sourcing fee and a final sourcing fee if you are happy to proceed. The fees are agreed with you before the service starts. If we cannot find a vehicle for you, the initial sourcing fee is refunded.

Our earnings from sourcing come from the agreed sourcing fees, not a markup added to the vehicle price. We explain the seller, price, fees, refund and cancellation terms before you commit. Your statutory rights are not affected.

What to tell us

  • The make and model you want, or two or three you would consider
  • Specification and options that matter to you
  • Your budget and the mileage you are happy with
  • Colour preferences, and anything you want to avoid
  • When you need the car
  • Whether you have a car to part exchange

Who you are buying from

From our own stock

Where a car is ours, we sell it to you as the seller and you buy from Alleyne Automotive Ltd.

Sourced on your behalf

We purchase on your behalf and help arrange the handover. The seller and purchase terms can vary and are confirmed in writing before you commit. If a dealership provides a warranty, we can help you liaise with them about it; the applicable warranty terms and your statutory rights remain in place.
