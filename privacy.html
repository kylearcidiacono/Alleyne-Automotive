Privacy notice

Last updated 20 November 2026

Who we are

Alleyne Automotive Ltd (company number 17513152, registered office 66 Paul Street, London, EC2A 4NA) is the controller of the personal data described here. You can contact us through the details on our contact page.

What we collect

When you enquire using the form, by phone, email or WhatsApp we receive what you send us. That can include your name, contact details, the car you are interested in, and details of a car you want to sell or part exchange such as its registration number, mileage and photos. If you buy or sell a car we also hold the records of that transaction.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

To answer your enquiry and to take steps you ask for before a contract: this is needed to deal with your request. To carry out a sale, purchase or part exchange with you: this is performing a contract. To keep records we must keep by law, for example tax and accounting records: this is a legal obligation. To protect our business and deal with disputes: this is our legitimate interest.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only where needed to do what you ask, for example with a delivery provider, and with our professional advisers. Our website uses Cloudflare for hosting and spam protection, and an email delivery provider to send form enquiries. WhatsApp is run by a third party, which has its own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for no longer than we need them for the enquiry. We keep records of sales and purchases for as long as the law requires, which is currently six years for accounting records.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your personal data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to move it. Contact us to do so. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
