Frequently asked questions.

Answers about sourcing, buying, viewings and delivery.

Do you sell from your own stock or only source cars?

We buy and sell cars and can source one to match your brief. We confirm who is selling the car and the agreed terms before you commit.

Is there a charge for sourcing?

There is an initial sourcing fee and a final sourcing fee if you are happy to proceed. If we cannot find a vehicle for you, the initial fee is refunded. We explain the fees and terms before you commit.

Do I need an appointment to view a car?

Yes. Viewings are by appointment only. Contact us to book a time.

Where are viewings held?

We confirm the viewing address when we book you in.

Do you deliver?

Yes, across the UK. The charge depends on the destination and is quoted before you commit.

Can I view a car at home?

For vehicles in our current stock, we can arrange a home viewing for an agreed fee. If you buy the vehicle, the fee is deducted from its price. We explain the terms before you book.

Is a deposit needed to reserve a car?

A deposit is required to hold a vehicle from our current stock. The amount depends on the vehicle and is agreed with you before you pay.

Can I part exchange my car?

Yes. Any figure is agreed after we have seen the car, and not before. If the car is on finance, tell us.

Which cars do you deal in?

We specialise in BMW, Audi and Volkswagen and can source any make or model. See the brands page.

What are my rights if I buy from you as a consumer?

Your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 apply. The car must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described.

What if something goes wrong?

Tell us. See our complaints page for how to do it.

Another question?

Ask us and we will come back to you.
