Complaints

Last updated 20 November 2026

How to complain

If something has gone wrong, tell us. Write to us using the details on our contact page and include your name, how to reach you, the car concerned, what happened and what you would like us to do.

What happens next

We will acknowledge your complaint, look into it and reply to you in writing.

If we cannot agree

If we cannot settle a complaint with you, we will tell you in writing whether we agree to use an approved alternative dispute resolution provider and, if so, which one.

Independent advice

You can get free advice about your consumer rights from Citizens Advice. Your statutory rights are not affected by this page.
