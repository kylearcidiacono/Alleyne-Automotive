How part exchange works

  1. 1

    Tell us about your car

    Make, model, year, mileage, registration and photos.

  2. 2

    We see it

    Any figure is agreed after we have seen the car, and not before.

  3. 3

    Put it towards the next one

    The agreed figure goes towards the car you are buying. We confirm everything in writing.

Good to know

No quoted valuations

We do not give figures from a description alone. The car has to be seen first.

Finance on your car

If you still owe money on your car, tell us. The settlement has to be cleared as part of the deal.
